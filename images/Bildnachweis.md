## Note on photographic substitutions

The three original period photographs used by the official catalogue are still
under copyright and are not reproduced here. Each has been replaced by a
freely licensed image of the same subject:

| Question | Original | Used instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `general_70` | Heinemann handing Schmidt his appointment certificate, 1974 | Bundesarchiv portrait of Gustav Heinemann |
| `general_181` | Brandt's genuflection at the Warsaw Ghetto memorial, 1970 | The memorial plaque at that site, depicting the genuflection |
| `general_235` | Mitterrand and Kohl at Verdun, 1984 | The two men shaking hands at the 1982 Copenhagen European Council |

The question text is unchanged and still describes the original event; the
images illustrate the subject rather than reproducing the historic frame.
