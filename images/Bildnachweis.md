## Images removed

Three questions in the official catalogue are illustrated with period press
photographs that remain in copyright. No freely licensed photograph of the
same scene exists, so rather than substitute a different picture — which would
make the question text describe something the image does not show — those
images were **removed** and the questions stand on their text alone:

| Question | Original image | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `general_70` | Heinemann presenting Schmidt with his appointment certificate, 1974 | removed — text only |
| `general_181` | Brandt's genuflection at the Warsaw Ghetto memorial, 1970 | removed — text only |
| `general_235` | Mitterrand and Kohl at Verdun, 1984 | removed — text only |

Question wording, answer options and answer keys are unchanged and remain
verbatim from the official catalogue. If a freely licensed photograph of any
of these events comes to light, it can be added back.
